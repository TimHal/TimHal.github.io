I teach tutorials in pattern recognition and computer vision at TU Dortmund University and co-supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses in document analysis and representation learning.

Looking for a thesis topic? If you are a Bachelor's or Master's student at TU Dortmund and something on my research page interests you, send me an email. Outline your (rough) project idea along with a short CV and transcript.

If you are already a student of mine: I have lunch at the Mensa every Friday at 11:30 with whoever wants to join, no agenda. Starting from my office at 11:15. Ideally send me a quick email in advance if you plan to join.
