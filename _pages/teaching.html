I teach tutorials in pattern recognition and computer vision at TU Dortmund University and co-supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses in document analysis and representation learning — seven so far.

Looking for a thesis topic? If you are a Bachelor's or Master's student at TU Dortmund and something on my research page interests you, send me an email — topics are usually shaped around what you want to learn rather than picked from a fixed list. A short note about your background and interests is enough to start.

If you are already a student of mine: I have lunch at the Mensa every Friday at 12:00 with whoever wants to join, no agenda.
