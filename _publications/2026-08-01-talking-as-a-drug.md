Conversational agents are increasingly proposed as companions for older adults. This interdisciplinary
position paper asks what changes if we describe such conversations the way medicine describes a
treatment: with a dosage, an expected effect, side effects, contraindications and a requirement of
informed consent — and who carries responsibility when the treatment is a model.
